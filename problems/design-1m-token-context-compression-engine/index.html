01
L4 · FUNDAMENTALS

R & E · Requirements, Little's Law & HBM Physics.

10,000 concurrent sessions averaging 1 turn every 10s = 1,000 turns/sec (2,500 peak). At 250k avg tokens/turn, raw ingress is 250M tokens/sec.

Illustrative Sizing: Compressing to 12k tokens cuts downstream prefill to 12M tok/s (20.8x reduction). Splitting 250k tokens into 62 parallel 4k chunks scored in W = 90 ms yields L = 1,000 * 0.090 = 90 concurrent turns (~5,580 chunks) served by 32–40 INT8 L40S/H100 workers.

L4 · FUNDAMENTALS

S & H · Tiered Storage Schema & HMAC Tenant Isolation.

Multi-tenant RAG and agent platforms cannot share KV-cache prefixes or summary trees across organizations.

Every Redis L1 and NVMe RocksDB L2 key is prefixed with HMAC_SHA256(cluster_secret, tenant_id). RadixAttention blocks on GPU pods are tagged with (tenant_hmac, model_epoch, anchor_sha256).

D & E · 3-3-3 Technology Trade-Offs & TCO Economics.

Without compression, 250M input tokens/sec costs ~$125/sec ($10.8M/day) in frontier prefill compute.

Compressing 20.8x to 12M tokens/sec cuts LLM prefill to ~$6.00/sec while the 40-GPU INT8 SLM fleet + Redis/RocksDB costs ~$0.04/sec—yielding >95% net TCO reduction.

L6 · SCALE & TRADE-OFFS

D · Failure Modes, Assumptions & Glossary.

Assumptions & Limitations: Assumes co-located SLM and LLM GPU pools in-region, 128k–1M input windows, and 8k–16k target budgets. Not suited for <8k prompts or verbatim legal redlines.

Failure Modes: (1) SLM fleet overload (>450 ms) trips Circuit Breaker OPEN to Pinned Anchors + 12k raw tail; (2) Redis L1 shard loss falls back to NVMe RocksDB L2 (+12 ms); (3) Anchor SHA-256 mismatch rejects with 403.
