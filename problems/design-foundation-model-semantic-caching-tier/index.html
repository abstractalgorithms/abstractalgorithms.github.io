01
L4 · FUNDAMENTALS

R & E · Requirements, Scale Targets & 100M Entry Sizing.

Targets: 5,000 RPS sustained, 15,000 RPS peak, >25% combined hit ratio, <15 ms p99 evaluation overhead, 100M active entries.

Sizing 100M 384-d SQ8 vectors = 38.4 GB + 14.4 GB HNSW edges (M=16) + 2.56 GB L1 Radix = 55.36 GB × 2 (RF=2) = 110.7 GB DRAM across 8 shards. Zstd-compressed payloads (2 KB avg) = 400 GB NVMe.

L4 · FUNDAMENTALS

S & H · Storage Entities & 5-Tier Decoupled Architecture.

Edge Gateway normalizes prompts and derives tenant_hash = HMAC-SHA256(master_key, tenant_id). L1 Radix Trie checks exact matches and emits GPU KV-cache pod affinity hints.

L2 queries sharded SQ8 HNSW with a Roaring Bitmap tenant pre-filter, then fetches the Zstd payload from NVMe RocksDB.

L5 · APPLIED DESIGN

A & D · RPC Contracts, Cosine Calibration & Slot Guard.

EvaluateCache accepts (tenant_id, model_id, model_epoch, system_prompt_hash, user_prompt, max_cosine_dist, deadline_ms) and returns hit_tier + cached_payload + kv_prefix_affinity.

Cosine distance calibration divides space into Safe (d < 0.08), Gray (0.08 <= d < 0.14), and Reject (d >= 0.14). An Entity/Negation Slot Guard enforces exact parity on numbers, dates, and negations.

L5 · APPLIED DESIGN

E · Technology Trade-Offs, Dynamic TTL & Epoch Invalidation.

HNSW SQ8 delivers 96.5% Recall@10 in 5.5 ms p99; DiskANN takes 18–25 ms on NVMe (violating SLA) and IVF-PQ distorts cosine distances near tau = 0.08.

Volatility-aware TTL assigns 7d to static docs, 1h to RAG knowledge, and 0s bypass to live data. Bumping model_epoch invalidates stale entries in O(1) without cluster-wide flushes.

L6 · SCALE & TRADE-OFFS

D · Failure Modes, Circuit Breakers, Assumptions & Glossary.

Three circuit breakers govern safety: Latency Breaker (>12 ms p99 opens L2 bypass), Drift Breaker (5% shadow LLM Judge drift >1.5% tightens tau from 0.08 to 0.05), and Queue Shedding (worker depth >64).

Assumptions & Limitations: Assumes low-temperature (T <= 0.3) workloads and canonicalizable prompts; high-entropy creative generation or strict real-time feeds must set bypass_cache = true.
