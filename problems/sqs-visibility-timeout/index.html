Misconfigured SQS visibility timeouts create a painful trade-off:

  1. Too Short (30 s): Healthy 48–110 s jobs get picked up by Worker 2 while Worker 1 is still charging the payment gateway.
  2. Too Long (900 s): If a Spot worker gets SIGTERM at t = 2 s, the invoice sits invisible for 15 minutes.

Calling ChangeMessageVisibility on the same thread as blocking OCR/HTTP I/O never fires when the job stalls. Even with a background heartbeat thread, SQS Standard guarantees at-least-once delivery—network partitions or Spot SIGKILL after charging the PSP (before DeleteMessage) will still redeliver the message.

STAGE 04 · ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONL6 · SCALE & TRADE-OFFS

Enterprise solution: T/2 Async Heartbeat + Idempotent Settlement Ledger.

Why this matters: Operational control: configure bounds, failure handling, and safe production defaults.

Combine fast crash detection, bounded heartbeats, and strict consumer idempotency:

  • 45 s Initial Visibility + 20 s Async Heartbeat: Set VisibilityTimeout = 45 s (crashed Spot workers recover in <= 45 s). A separate background timer calls ChangeMessageVisibility(+45s) every 20 s (T/2) up to MAX_JOB_DURATION = 900 s, always cancelled in finally.
  • Idempotent Consumer Ledger + PSP Key: Before calling the payment gateway, acquire a conditional DB row INSERT INTO settled_invoices (invoice_id, status) ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING (or IN_PROGRESS lease) and pass invoice_id as the PSP Idempotency-Key.

05
STAGE 05 · SYSTEM DESIGN APPLICATIONSL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

Where visibility leases and idempotent consumers apply.

Why this matters: Scope boundary: identify where this pattern fits best and where simpler or async alternatives win.

Lease-extension + idempotency is the universal pattern for variable-duration async workers:

  • Document OCR, Video Transcoding & LLM Batch Jobs: Keeps crash recovery at 45s even for 15-minute jobs.
  • Billing & Payout Workers: Pairs queue leases with DB unique constraints and PSP idempotency keys.
  • Spot / Preemptible Compute Fleets: Handles SIGTERM gracefully by cancelling heartbeats and letting VT expire cleanly.
