The idea to keep with you

When a single bottleneck threatens end-to-end availability, decompose 1-hour sliding windows into sixty 1-minute tumbling HLL + CMS sketches so workers merge fixed kilobyte arrays in <1 MB RAM.

TL;DR30-second simplified summary
▾

At 1M events/sec across 50M unique IDs, exact HashSets across 60 overlapping panes hit 18 GB/pod (OOMKilled 137). Decomposing the 1-hour window into 60 1-minute tumbling HLL (16 KB) and Count-Min + Min-Heap (256 KB) panes cuts state to <1 MB/shard, isolates whale tenants, and shrinks checkpoints to <150 ms.

HyperLogLog & Count-Min Sketch (Streaming Sketches)
HyperLogLog tracks max leading-zero runs across m registers to estimate distinct counts in ~1.5–12 KB, while Count-Min Sketch uses d hash rows x w counters to bound item frequencies in sub-megabyte RAM.
