Three alerting anti-patterns compound during real incidents:

  1. Noise Habituation: 60 pages/day train responders to blindly acknowledge notifications.
  2. Downstream Cascade Flood: One Postgres failure fires 47 gateway/upstream 504 pages simultaneously.
  3. HA Pair Duplicates: Active-active Prometheus pairs page twice without deterministic dedup_key routing.

STAGE 04 · ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONL6 · SCALE & TRADE-OFFS

Enterprise solution: Multi-Window Burn Rates, Inhibition, and Dedup Keys.

Why this matters: Operational control: configure bounds, failure handling, and safe production defaults.

Cut weekly pages from 420 to < 15 while keeping P1 MTTD <= 2 minutes:

  • Multi-Window, Multi-Burn-Rate Rules (99.9% SLO -> 0.001 budget): Page immediately on 14.4x burn (2% of 30-day budget in 1h) only when BOTH 1h AND 5m windows exceed 14.4 * 0.001—catching hard drops in <= 2m while resetting fast on recovery.
  • Slow-Burn Ticket Tier: Route 1x–3x burn (6h & 30m, or 3d & 6h) to Slack/Jira tickets instead of PagerDuty.
  • Alertmanager Grouping & inhibit_rules: Group by (cluster, service) with group_wait: 30s, group_interval: 5m, inhibit downstream 504 pages when root-cause PostgresCritical fires, and hash (alertname, cluster, service) into PagerDuty dedup_key.
Multi-Window Burn Routing

Error Budget Burn Rate Escalation Tiers

Requiring both long and short windows to breach eliminates blip noise and clears pages immediately upon recovery.

groups:
  - name: checkout_slo_burn_rate
    rules:
      - record: job:http_requests:error_ratio5m
        expr: |
          sum by (cluster, service) (rate(http_requests_total{status=~"5.."}[5m]))
          / sum by (cluster, service) (rate(http_requests_total[5m]))
      - alert: CheckoutSLOBurnRateCritical
        expr: |
          job:http_requests:error_ratio1h{service="checkout"} > (14.4 * 0.001)
          and
          job:http_requests:error_ratio5m{service="checkout"} > (14.4 * 0.001)
        for: 2m
        labels:
          severity: page

STAGE 05 · SYSTEM DESIGN APPLICATIONSL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

Where SLO burn-rate alerting governs production reliability.

Why this matters: Scope boundary: identify where this pattern fits best and where simpler or async alternatives win.

Error-budget alerting is the standard SRE operating model for high-scale services:

  • Payments & Checkout APIs: Pages only when customer-visible availability or latency SLOs burn dangerously fast.
  • Automated Canary Rollbacks: Uses the same 5m burn-rate recording rules to abort bad deployments.
  • Multi-Region HA Observability: Uses Alertmanager dedup_key and inhibit_rules across redundant Prometheus pairs.
