Throughput Math & 420 TB/Day -> 140 TB/Day Yield Funnel.

Why this matters: Derive sustained/peak rollout QPS, H100 cluster sizing, and storage compression ratios from the 100M traces/day target.

With a 40% combined verification + PRM yield, 100M golden traces/day requires 250M raw rollouts/day (250,000,000 / 86,400 = 2,894 rollouts/sec average, 7,500 peak with 2.6x burst headroom). Across 2,048 H100 GPUs (256x 8-GPU HGX nodes) running FP8 + PagedAttention, each GPU sustains ~1.4–3.7 rollouts/sec.

def pipeline_rates(target_golden_per_day: int = 100_000_000, yield_rate: float = 0.40) -> dict[str, float]:
    if not (0.0 < yield_rate <= 1.0):
        raise ValueError("yield_rate must be in (0, 1]")
    raw_per_day = target_golden_per_day / yield_rate
    return {
        "raw_rollouts_per_sec": round(raw_per_day / 86_400, 1),
        "golden_commits_per_sec": round(target_golden_per_day / 86_400, 1),
    }

Stage 5 · Intermediateintermediate

Streaming MinHash LSH & 13-Gram Decontamination.

Why this matters: Filter semantic near-duplicates and benchmark contamination at 1,157+ traces/sec with <=4ms p99 latency.

Think of Streaming MinHash LSH & 13-Gram Decontamination as the intuition layer for Design a 100M-Trace/Day Enterprise Synthetic Data & Self-Play Pipeline: Filter semantic near-duplicates and benchmark contamination at 1,157+ traces/sec with <=4ms p99 latency.

Stateless decontamination workers hold an immutable, memory-mapped 13-gram Bloom filter containing all held-out evaluation benchmarks (MMLU, HumanEval, SWE-bench, GSM8K, MATH) alongside a distributed 128-perm MinHash LSH index (32 bands x 4 rows, J >= 0.80 drop threshold).

Decontamination

Dual-Stage LSH + 13-Gram Bloom Filter

1.Zero Eval Leakage: Any 13-gram overlap with held-out benchmarks immediately quarantines the trace.

2.Hot-Reloadable Filter: New benchmark releases are compiled into a shadow Bloom filter and swapped atomically.

Caption: Streaming LSH and 13-gram Bloom checks purge duplicates and benchmark leaks in under 4ms.

PRM Rejection Sampling & Early-Stop Tree Search.

Why this matters: Score intermediate reasoning steps at <=35ms p95 and prune low-reward branches mid-generation.

Think of PRM Rejection Sampling & Early-Stop Tree Search as the intuition layer for Design a 100M-Trace/Day Enterprise Synthetic Data & Self-Play Pipeline: Score intermediate reasoning steps at <=35ms p95 and prune low-reward branches mid-generation.

A dedicated PRM inference tier evaluates step-level reward r_t in [0, 1] at <= 35 ms p95 per step. During multi-path tree search, partial trajectories dropping below 0.78 are pruned immediately, and full traces must satisfy min_t(r_t) >= 0.78 before entering Apache Iceberg.

PRM Gate

Step-Level Scoring & Early Branch Pruning

1.Early Pruning: Killing dead-end reasoning branches mid-rollout saves ~25–35% of H100 decode FLOPs.

2.Calibration Audit: Log score histograms to ClickHouse to detect PRM drift across domains.

Caption: PRM step scoring enforces high reasoning quality and cuts wasted decode compute via early pruning.
