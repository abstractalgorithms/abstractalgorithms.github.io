The idea to keep with you

When traffic spikes expose hidden coupling, fix both write-side micro-batch cadence (30s -> 5m) and read-side maintenance (data files + manifests + snapshot expiry) with bounded concurrency.

TL;DR30-second simplified summary
▾

30-second commits across 40 partitions generated 200K small files and 50K manifests, causing 45s planning stalls and S3 503s. Remediate by shifting to 5-minute hash-distributed writes, draining the backlog partition-by-partition with partial-progress=true, running rewrite_manifests + expire_snapshots, and enabling object-storage hash prefixes.
