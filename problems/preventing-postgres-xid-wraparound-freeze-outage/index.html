Stage 1: Incident Anatomy — 30K/s Ledger & Emergency Write Freeze.

Why this matters: Trace how 30,000 updates/sec combined with pinned xmin and throttled autovacuum drives a 400 GB ledger to a read-only halt.

At 30,000 write XIDs/sec, PostgreSQL consumes ~108M XIDs per hour and hits the 2B wraparound limit in ~18.5 hours if relfrozenxid cannot advance. Meanwhile, unreclaimed dead tuples inflate a 400 GB table to 2 TB (5x bloat), degrading buffer-cache hit ratios and slowing every sequential/index scan until the emergency shutdown triggers.

FOUNDATION · INCIDENT TIMELINE

How 30,000 Updates/Sec Hits the 2B XID Wall in < 19 Hours

1.18.5-Hour Runway: At 30K XIDs/sec, even a half-day autovacuum stall pushes a table close to the 2B limit.

2.Compounding Slowdown: 5x bloat makes any eventual VACUUM scan 5x slower to complete.

At 30,000 updates/sec, pinned xmin and throttled vacuum bloat tables 5x and exhaust the 2B XID runway in under 19 hours.

Stage 2: Live Triage Queries (pg_stat_activity & pg_class).

Why this matters: Identify the exact oldest relfrozenxid tables, idle-in-transaction blockers, and inactive replication slots during an incident.

During an active XID-age spike, operators must immediately rank tables by age(relfrozenxid) in pg_class and cross-reference pg_stat_activity for backend_xmin holders and pg_replication_slots for inactive xmin/catalog_xmin pins. Terminating a single zombie PID via pg_terminate_backend(pid) unblocks autovacuum within seconds.

FOUNDATION · LIVE TRIAGE

Three-Query Incident Triage to Unpin Global xmin

1.Check Subtransactions: Monitor pg_stat_activity wait events for SubtransSLRU contention alongside backend_xmin.

2.Safe Slot Drop: Verify consumer replica is decommissioned before calling pg_drop_replication_slot().

Inspecting pg_class, pg_stat_activity, and pg_replication_slots pinpoints the exact blocker holding back relfrozenxid.

Stage 3: Enforcing Session Timeouts & Replication WAL Caps.

Why this matters: Prevent future xmin pinning automatically using 30s idle-in-transaction timeouts and max_slot_keep_wal_size.

Manual triage is too slow at 30K XIDs/sec. Setting idle_in_transaction_session_timeout = '30s' and statement_timeout = '15s' on OLTP/reporting roles automatically aborts forgotten BEGIN; SELECT sessions. Setting max_slot_keep_wal_size = '20GB' invalidates disconnected replication slots before they pin xmin or fill pg_wal.

INTERMEDIATE · BLOCKER PREVENTION

Automated Guardrails Against Zombie Sessions & Dead Slots

1.Replica Read Isolation: Route long-running BI analytics to a dedicated read replica without hot_standby_feedback.

2.PgBouncer Transaction Pooling: Transaction-mode pooling returns connections immediately after COMMIT, reducing idle-in-tx risk.

30-second idle-in-transaction timeouts and max_slot_keep_wal_size prevent zombie sessions and dead slots from pinning xmin.

Stage 4: HOT Updates with fillfactor=70 on Update-Heavy Tables.

Why this matters: Keep 30,000 updates/sec page-local and hold table bloat under 5% using fillfactor=70.

On the 400 GB ledger, setting ALTER TABLE ledger SET (fillfactor = 70); reserves 30% free space on every 8 KB heap page. Non-indexed column updates stay on the same heap page as Heap-Only Tuples (HOT), eliminating secondary B-tree index writes and allowing inline page pruning to keep steady-state bloat under 5%.

INTERMEDIATE · BLOAT CONTROL

fillfactor=70 + Inline Page Pruning for < 5% Bloat

1.REPACK / pg_repack: Existing 5x-bloated tables require online pg_repack (or VACUUM FULL) after setting fillfactor=70 to rewrite dense pages.

2.Index Column Hygiene: Avoid indexing monotonically updated timestamp/counter columns that would disqualify HOT updates.

Reserving 30% free space per page with fillfactor=70 enables HOT updates and inline pruning, holding bloat under 5%.

intermediate

Stage 5: Autovacuum Cost-Limit & Worker Scaling.

Why this matters: Configure autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit=2000 and multi-worker parallelism so freezing outpaces XID burn.

Default autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit=200 caps vacuum at ~8 MB/s per worker—far too slow for a 400 GB table at 30K updates/sec. Increasing autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit=2000 (with cost_delay=2ms), lowering autovacuum_vacuum_scale_factor=0.01, and setting autovacuum_max_workers=6 gives freeze workers 10x I/O throughput without starving OLTP queries.

INTERMEDIATE · VACUUM THROUGHPUT

10x Autovacuum I/O Budget & Scale-Factor Tuning

1.Shared Cost Balance: Remember autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit is shared across active workers unless overridden per table.

2.maintenance_work_mem: Set maintenance_work_mem=1GB..2GB so VACUUM stores dead-tuple TIDs in 1 pass instead of multiple index scans.

Tuning cost_limit=2000, scale_factor=0.01, and maintenance_work_mem=1GB lets autovacuum keep pace with 30K updates/sec.

Stage 6: Partitioning & Subtransaction Avoidance at 30K/s.

Why this matters: Bound freeze scan blast radius via time-based partitioning and avoid SubtransSLRU XID amplification.

Vacuuming a monolithic 400 GB table takes hours even with tuned I/O; range-partitioning by day or week limits active vacuuming to ~20 GB hot partitions while cold partitions stay 100% all-frozen in the Visibility Map. Additionally, eliminating nested SAVEPOINT / PL/pgSQL EXCEPTION blocks avoids subtransaction XID burn and SubtransSLRU lock contention.

ADVANCED · PARTITIONING & SUBTX

Bounding Freeze Blast Radius with Partitioned Hot Tables

1.95% I/O Reduction: Partitioning shrinks active freeze scans from 400 GB to a 20 GB hot partition (~4.3 minutes).

2.Subtransaction Trap: A single transaction with 1,000 SAVEPOINTs burns 1,000 XIDs and can trigger SubtransSLRU stalls.

Range partitioning shrinks freeze scans from 400 GB to 20 GB hot partitions while avoiding SAVEPOINT XID amplification.

advanced

Stage 7: Tiered XID-Age Alerting & Single-User Emergency Runbook.

Why this matters: Catch wraparound risk 10+ hours early via Prometheus telemetry and document safe single-user VACUUM FREEZE recovery.

Export max(age(relfrozenxid)) and max(age(datfrozenxid)) every 60 seconds with alerts at 150M (info), 500M (page on-call), and 1.5B (critical). If a cluster ever reaches the 2B read-only wall, stop application pools, start postgres --single -D $PGDATA <db>, execute VACUUM FREEZE;, and restart normal multi-user service.

ADVANCED · TELEMETRY & RECOVERY

Tiered XID-Age Alerting & Single-User Emergency Thaw

1.Database-Wide datfrozenxid: Remember pg_database.datfrozenxid tracks the oldest relfrozenxid across all tables—even unused template0/postgres DBs.

2.Pre-Freeze Verification: Confirm disk space headroom before running VACUUM FREEZE so WAL generation does not fill $PGDATA.

60-second XID-age telemetry catches wraparound hours early, while single-user VACUUM FREEZE provides deterministic recovery.
