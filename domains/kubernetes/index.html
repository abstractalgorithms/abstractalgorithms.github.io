PRODUCTION INCIDENTSL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

CrashLoopBackOff, PDB deadlocks & zero-downtime drains.

Diagnose pod lifecycle stalls and cluster-autoscaler blockers.

Why do clients briefly see 502s during a rolling deploy even with graceful SIGTERM handling?

Endpoint removal from kube-proxy/Ingress happens asynchronously in parallel with SIGTERM. Adding a preStop: sleep 5 hook gives load balancers time to drain before the app stops listening.

Why should Liveness probes never check external dependencies like Postgres or Redis?

If Postgres blips for 10 seconds, Liveness kills and restarts every healthy app pod simultaneously, turning a minor DB hiccup into a full outage.

Theory & Tips and Tricks

Practical heuristics & interview traps

  • 01Never include downstream DB/cache checks in a Liveness probe—only in Readiness.
  • 02Add a short preStop sleep (e.g., 5s) before handling SIGTERM so Ingress/kube-proxy stops routing new connections first.
  • 03Set TopologySpreadConstraints and PodDisruptionBudget on production deployments so a single AZ or node drain doesn't drop capacity.
