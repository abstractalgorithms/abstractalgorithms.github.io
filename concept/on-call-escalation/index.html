01
STAGE 01 · WHAT IT ISL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

What is an On-Call Escalation Policy.

On-call escalation is the deterministic human-routing state machine behind your monitoring stack. Every paging alert maps to a single owning team in a service catalog (Backstage/PagerDuty) with a runbook_url and severity tier.

A standard 4-tier ladder gives L1 Primary 5 minutes to acknowledge via push/SMS/voice bypass before escalating to L2 Secondary (5–10m), L3 Engineering Manager / Domain Lead (10–20m), and L4 Director / VP (20m+). On SEV-1/SEV-2 incidents, the Incident Command System (ICS) separates the Incident Commander (IC) (coordinating timelines and communications) from SMEs (debugging and mitigating).

STAGE 02 · WHY WE NEED ITL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

Why we need On-Call Escalation (and why Slack channels fail).

Without deterministic escalation, critical 2 AM outages sit in unmonitored Slack channels for 40 minutes while teams guess who owns a failing database or payment gateway. Conversely, routing non-urgent warnings (disk at 75%, flaky staging tests) to pages causes alert fatigue: engineers blindly ACK pages and go back to sleep, missing real SEV-1s.

Independent Notification Pipeline (Dead Man's Switch)

If an AWS region outage knocks out your self-hosted Alertmanager or Slack webhook, nobody gets paged—host paging on an independent SaaS (PagerDuty/Opsgenie) with a continuous heartbeat check.

Toil Budget & 48-Hour Alert Hygiene

Cap off-hours pages at <= 2 per 7-day shift; any alert that fires without requiring immediate human action must be fixed or deleted within 48 hours.

STAGE 03 · WHERE IT IS USEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

Where On-Call Escalation applies (and where to avoid paging).

Reserve high-urgency 24/7 paging for user-facing SLO burn and data-loss risks; route everything else to async tickets or automated self-healing.

04
STAGE 04 · HOW IT IS MANAGEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

How Escalation Policies & Runbook Contracts are managed.

Manage schedules and escalation policies as code (e.g., Terraform pagerduty_escalation_policy) synced to HR/Okta groups so deactivated employees never leave dead-end gaps, and enforce runbook_url, severity, and team labels on every Alertmanager rule in CI.

# Prometheus Alertmanager rule + PagerDuty escalation contract
groups:
  - name: payments-slo
    rules:
      - alert: PaymentsHighErrorBurn
        expr: job:http_5xx_burn:ratio_5m > 14.4 * 0.001 and job:http_5xx_burn:ratio_1h > 14.4 * 0.001
        for: 2m
        labels:
          severity: sev1
          team: payments-core
        annotations:
          summary: "Payments error budget burning >14.4x"
          runbook_url: "https://runbooks.internal/payments/high-error-burn"

STAGE 06 · PRODUCTION ARCHITECTUREL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

Production example: 02:14 AM SEV-1 Escalation & Shift Handoff.

At 02:14 AM, Alertmanager groups 12 pod alerts into one PagerDuty incident. L1 is commuting and misses the 5-minute ACK; PagerDuty escalates to L2, who ACKs, declares SEV-1, and triggers Incident.io to page the IC and open a bridge while SMEs isolate the bad deploy.
