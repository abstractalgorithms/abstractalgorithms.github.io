CAP Theorem & Consistency Models

Reason about consistency, availability, and partition tolerance—and choose linearizable, sequential, or eventual models deliberately.

XLinkedInReddit
intermediate
Concept Breakdown

💡 Why It Matters

Misunderstanding CAP leads to silent data loss, double spends, and “always available” APIs that lie during splits.

🌐 Real-World Production Usages & Examples

Banking ledger during partition

Scenario: Two regions lose connectivity during a transfer spike.

Outcome: CP design may reject writes; AP design may expose divergent balances until merge.

✓ When to Use

  • •Multi-region databases
  • •Caches in front of authoritative stores
  • •Leader-based replication designs

✕ When NOT to Use

  • •Single-node databases with no partition risk
  • •Batch analytics with no online SLA

🔑 Core Architecture & Rules

  • 1CP vs AP is a partition-time trade-off
  • 2Consistency models define client-visible ordering
  • 3Quorum math implements chosen guarantees

⚖️ Architectural Trade-Off Analysis

Strong consistency

PROSimpler application logic
CONHigher latency and availability hits during partitions

⚠️ Failure Modes & Mitigations

Split-brain writes

Two leaders accept conflicting writes.

Mitigation: Fencing tokens, epoch numbers, or quorum leases.
