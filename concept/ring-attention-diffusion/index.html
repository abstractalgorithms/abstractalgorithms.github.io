STAGE 01 · SPATIO-TEMPORAL SCALINGfoundation

Why 1080p Video DiTs Exceed Single-GPU HBM.

Why this matters: Shard 243K-token 1080p video DiT sequences across 8 GPUs because single-GPU HBM cannot hold full attention matrices.

A 5-second 1080p video at 24 fps compressed 8x spatially and 4x temporally yields ~243,000 latent patch tokens.

Materializing an unsharded 243K x 243K attention score matrix in FP16 would require > 118 GB per head—far beyond an 80 GB HBM3 GPU.

STAGE 01 · SPATIO-TEMPORAL SCALING

Why 1080p Video DiTs Exceed Single-GPU HBM

1.Quadratic Attention: 243K x 243K attention scores exceed 80 GB HBM3 on a single GPU even before activations.

2.Sequence Parallelism: Splitting Q, K, V evenly across 8 GPUs keeps each GPU's local working set to ~30.4K tokens.

3.Zero Approximation: Ring Attention computes exact full-sequence attention—no sparse window quality loss.

Sharding 243K video patch tokens across 8 H100s via Ring Attention eliminates single-GPU HBM OOMs without approximation.

STAGE 02 · RING ATTENTIONfoundation

Overlapping P2P NVLink KV Transfers with FP8 FlashAttention.

Why this matters: Overlap peer-to-peer NVLink KV shard transfers with FP8 FlashAttention-3 compute across the 8-GPU ring.

Each of the 8 GPUs holds 1/8 of the Q, K, V sequence (~30.4K tokens). In step s of the ring, GPU i computes local FlashAttention-3 against its current KV shard while asynchronously sending that KV shard to GPU (i + 1) % 8 over NVLink.

Because compute (5.21 ms) exceeds NVLink transfer (0.41 ms), communication is 100% hidden behind compute.

STAGE 02 · RING ATTENTION

Overlapping P2P NVLink KV Transfers with FP8 FlashAttention

1.Double Buffering: Each GPU computes FlashAttention on shard s while receiving shard s+1 into a spare buffer.

2.NVLink Bandwidth: 900 GB/s NVLink lets KV transfer finish faster than the O(L^2) attention compute.

3.FP8 Tensor Cores: FP8 quantization doubles Tensor Core throughput and halves KV ring transfer volume.

Double-buffered P2P NVLink transfers hide communication latency behind FP8 FlashAttention-3 compute.

STAGE 03 · EXACT MERGINGintermediate

Combining Partial Ring Attention Blocks via Online Softmax.

Why this matters: Combine partial attention outputs across ring steps exactly using online softmax log-sum-exp rescaling.

import math

def update_online_softmax(acc_out, acc_lse, block_out, block_lse):
    """Exact FlashAttention / RingAttention running log-sum-exp merge."""
    if acc_out is None:
        return block_out, block_lse
    new_lse = max(acc_lse, block_lse) + math.log1p(math.exp(-abs(acc_lse - block_lse)))
    w_acc = math.exp(acc_lse - new_lse)
    w_blk = math.exp(block_lse - new_lse)
    merged = [w_acc * a + w_blk * b for a, b in zip(acc_out, block_out)]
    return merged, new_lse
STAGE 03 · EXACT MERGING

Combining Partial Ring Attention Blocks via Online Softmax

1.Why Simple Averaging Fails: Each KV shard has a different local softmax denominator; simple averaging corrupts attention weights.

2.Log-Sum-Exp Trick: Tracking running max and log-sum-exp rescales prior accumulators identically to global softmax.

3.Exact Equivalence: Outputs match single-GPU full-matrix softmax up to floating-point rounding.

Online softmax log-sum-exp rescaling merges partial ring blocks into an exact global attention output.

4–8 Step LCM Distillation & CFG Batching.

Why this matters: Cut diffusion forward passes from 50 steps to 4-8 steps using LCM distillation and batched CFG.

Think of 4–8 Step LCM Distillation & CFG Batching as the intuition layer for Distributed Diffusion Inference: Ring Attention & LCM Distillation: Cut diffusion forward passes from 50 steps to 4-8 steps using LCM distillation and batched CFG.

Standard DDIM/Euler diffusion needs 30–50 sequential forward passes, and Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) doubles batch compute (conditional + unconditional). LCM / Consistency Distillation maps noisy latents directly toward the ODE trajectory endpoint in 4–8 steps, while CFG distillation folds guidance into a single pass.

STAGE 04 · STEP REDUCTION

4–8 Step LCM Distillation & CFG Batching

1.Step Distillation: Latent Consistency Models jump directly along the probability-flow ODE in 4-8 steps.

2.CFG Batching: Concatenating conditional and unconditional prompts into one batch doubles GPU utilization.

3.Quality vs. Steps: 4-8 distilled steps preserve sharp motion coherence while cutting compute 8x.

LCM distillation and batched Classifier-Free Guidance cut denoising from 50 steps to 4-8 steps.

Non-Blocking TinyVAE Latent Previews over SSE.

Why this matters: Stream progressive WebP previews at steps 1, 2, 4, and 8 using a non-blocking TinyVAE side stream.

Think of Non-Blocking TinyVAE Latent Previews over SSE as the intuition layer for Distributed Diffusion Inference: Ring Attention & LCM Distillation: Stream progressive WebP previews at steps 1, 2, 4, and 8 using a non-blocking TinyVAE side stream.

At denoising steps 1, 2, 4, and 8, copy the intermediate latent tensor via non-blocking cudaMemcpyAsync to a side stream running a 2M-parameter TinyVAE decoder (~18 ms), emitting progressive WebP frames over Redis Pub/Sub -> SSE while the main DiT keeps denoising uninterrupted.

STAGE 05 · PROGRESSIVE STREAMING

Non-Blocking TinyVAE Latent Previews over SSE

1.Zero Main-Loop Stall: Copying the small latent tensor to a side CUDA stream keeps the 8-GPU ring moving at full speed.

2.TinyVAE Speed: A 2M-parameter distilled decoder renders a draft WebP frame in ~18 ms vs. 400 ms for full VAE.

3.User Experience: Users see composition form within ~1.5s instead of staring at a blank spinner for 30s.

A side-stream TinyVAE decodes intermediate latents over SSE without stalling the main Ring Attention loop.

FP8 Tensor Cores, `torch.compile` & Static-Shape Bucketing

1.Why Bucketing Matters: Dynamic shapes trigger expensive torch.compile recompilations; 4-6 buckets cover all aspect ratios.

2.CUDA Graph Speedup: Capturing the static DiT step graph removes thousands of micro-kernel CPU launch overheads.

3.Weight Caching: Keeping 30 GB FP8 DiT weights on local NVMe RAID-0 avoids slow S3 cold downloads.

Resolution bucketing, FP8 torch.compile, and static CUDA Graphs boost DiT inference throughput by ~2.1x.

STAGE 07 · FAILURE MODES

NVLink Ring Desync, Spot Preemption & Latent Checkpointing

1.Preemption Savings: Checkpointing latents every 2 steps saves up to 75% of GPU compute when a spot node is reclaimed.

2.Ring Desync Guard: Set strict NCCL heartbeat timeouts so one dead GPU aborts the 8-GPU gang instead of hanging.

3.Idempotent Output: Write final MP4/WebP to S3 with job_id key so resumed jobs never produce duplicate charges.

Every-2-step latent checkpoints and strict NCCL watchdogs recover spot video jobs without losing prior denoising work.
