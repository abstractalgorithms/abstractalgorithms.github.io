Idempotency & Message Deduplication

Make retries safe with idempotency keys, dedupe stores, and exactly-once effects at the business layer.

Orientation & Intuition

At-least-once delivery is the practical default; idempotent handlers and deduplication tables turn duplicate deliveries into harmless no-ops.

Key takeaway Design every externally visible side effect to be idempotent or keyed—retries are not edge cases, they are the transport model.

Core Architecture & Trade-offs

Payment double-charges, duplicate shipments, and poison retries are almost always missing idempotency boundaries.

Key Architecture Invariants

  • Idempotency keys stored with TTL
  • Dedupe window must exceed max retry horizon
  • Outbox pairs DB write with publish intent

When to Use

  • HTTP APIs with retries
  • Kafka consumers
  • Saga compensations
  • Webhook receivers

When NOT to Use

  • Pure read-only queries with no side effects
  • Batch jobs that never retry

Deep Dive & Production Considerations

Safe retries

Transport is at-least-once; business logic must be idempotent.
