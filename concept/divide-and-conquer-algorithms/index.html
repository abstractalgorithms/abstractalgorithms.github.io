Divide & Conquer Algorithms

Decompose problems into independent subproblems, solve recursively, and combine results for merge sort, quickselect, and parallel speedups.

✓ When to Use

  • ✓Sorted subarray problems
  • ✓Recursive tree problems
  • ✓Parallel map-reduce style workloads

✕ When NOT to Use

  • ✕Heavy overlapping subproblems without memoization (use DP instead)
  • ✕Tiny inputs where recursion overhead dominates
