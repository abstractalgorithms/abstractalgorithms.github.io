STAGE 01 · WHAT IT ISL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

What are Star Schemas, Fact Grain & Dimensions.

Dimensional modeling (Kimball) structures analytical warehouses around business processes rather than OLTP 3NF normalization. Numerical measurements sit in long, narrow Fact tables at a single declared grain (e.g., 1 row per checkout line item), surrounded by wide, denormalized Dimension tables (dim_product, dim_customer, dim_date) linked via deterministic surrogate keys.

Fact tables come in three types: Transaction (fully additive events), Periodic Snapshot (semi-additive daily balances), and Accumulating Snapshot (workflow milestones like ordered_at -> shipped_at -> delivered_at).

02
STAGE 02 · WHY WE NEED ITL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

Why 3NF Fails Analytics (and How Star Schemas + SCD2 Prevent Double-Counting).

Querying normalized 3NF tables directly forces 8-way joins, causes silent fan-out double-counting when joining headers to line items, and breaks historical attribution when a customer changes loyalty tier. Star Schemas need only 1-hop joins, and SCD Type 2 (valid_from, valid_to, is_current) locks in the customer's tier at purchase time.

Star Schema Topology

1-Hop Star Join: fct_order_items -> Conformed Dimensions

Denormalizing hierarchies into flat dimensions keeps BI queries to fast 1-hop joins while SCD Type 2 preserves point-in-time history.

E-Commerce Star Schema in BigQuery + dbt

Replacing 14 normalized MySQL tables with fct_order_items (line-item grain) + dim_product + dim_customer (SCD2) + dim_date cut Looker p95 from 18s to 1.4s and eliminated fan-out revenue bugs.

Accumulating Snapshot for Fulfillment SLAs

fct_order_fulfillment (1 row/order) tracks ordered_at, packed_at, shipped_at, and delivered_at via incremental dbt merge, making SLA lag queries trivial.

STAGE 03 · WHERE IT IS USEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

Where Dimensional Modeling Fits (and Where 3NF / Graphs Win).

Use Kimball Star Schemas for enterprise BI and governed metrics; keep 3NF for high-concurrency OLTP and graph engines for recursive fraud rings.

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
Use: Enterprise Warehouses & Lakehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Iceberg)Fast columnar BI aggregations, conformed KPIs across Finance/Marketing, SCD2 history.Requires ETL/dbt modeling and semantic-layer tagging for semi-additive measures.
Avoid: High-Concurrency Row-Level OLTP3NF prevents update anomalies during concurrent transactional writes.Denormalized dimensions are expensive to update in-place on OLTP primaries.
Avoid: Multi-Hop Recursive Graph TraversalsUse Neo4j / graph engines for 6-hop fraud ring queries.SQL self-joins on star schemas scale poorly for deep graph traversals.
04
STAGE 04 · HOW IT IS MANAGEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

How to manage Grain, Semi-Additive Measures & Late Dimensions.

  1. Never Mix Grains: Don't put order-level shipping_cost on line-item rows without allocating it proportionally.
  2. Handle Orphan Facts: Map unresolved late-arriving dimension keys to a -1 ('Unknown') surrogate row and use LEFT JOIN in BI layers.
  3. Tag Semi-Additive Balances: Never SUM(account_balance) across days—use LAST_VALUE over time and SUM across accounts.
  4. Partition by Date: Partition large facts by date_key and cluster/Z-order by product_sk or tenant_id.
-- Point-in-time SCD Type 2 join locking customer loyalty tier at purchase time
SELECT
    d.fiscal_quarter,
    c.loyalty_tier AS tier_at_purchase,
    SUM(f.net_revenue_cents) / 100.0 AS net_revenue
FROM fct_order_items f
JOIN dim_date d ON f.date_key = d.date_key
LEFT JOIN dim_customer_scd2 c
    ON f.customer_id = c.customer_id
   AND f.order_ts >= c.valid_from
   AND f.order_ts < COALESCE(c.valid_to, TIMESTAMP '9999-12-31 00:00:00')
GROUP BY 1, 2;

Alternatives: Star Schema vs. Snowflake vs. One Big Table (OBT) & SCD Types.

Compare schema shapes and SCD strategies on storage cost, join speed, and backfill pain.

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs

STAGE 06 · PRODUCTION ARCHITECTUREL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

Production Architecture: SCD Type 2 Versioning & dbt Incremental Marts.

In dbt + Parquet/Iceberg, model stg_ -> int_ -> fct_/dim_, generate deterministic MD5/SHA256 surrogate keys, materialize large facts as incremental (merge / insert_overwrite on date_key), and assert zero overlapping SCD2 [valid_from, valid_to) windows in CI.

SCD Type 2 Lifecycle

Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2 Row Versioning

Closing old rows with valid_to = change_ts and inserting a new current row preserves exact point-in-time attribution.
