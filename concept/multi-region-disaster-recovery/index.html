STAGE 01 · WHAT IT ISL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

What is Multi-Region Disaster Recovery (RPO & RTO).

Multi-region disaster recovery (DR) protects stateful systems against correlated regional failures—bad control-plane pushes, fiber cuts, DNS/IAM outages, or ransomware—by replicating data to a secondary region.

Two metrics dictate the architecture:

  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective): maximum acceptable data loss (dictates async snapshots vs. WAL shipping vs. Aurora Global DB <1–5 s lag).
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective): maximum acceptable downtime (dictates Backup & Restore hours vs. Pilot Light 10–30 min vs. Warm Standby 1–5 min).
DR Failover Lifecycle

Primary Region Outage -> Health Check -> DNS & DB Promotion

Low DNS TTLs (30–60 s) or Global Accelerator Anycast prevent client DNS caches from pinning traffic to a dead region.

02
STAGE 02 · WHY WE NEED ITL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

Why untested DR plans fail during real regional outages.

Even 3+ Availability Zones within one region share regional control planes, IAM/DNS dependencies, and fiber trunks.

When a major region fails, two hidden blockers break untested DR plans: Cold-Start Capacity & Quota Exhaustion (thousands of customers fail over simultaneously and hit EC2/GPU quota ceilings) and Stale IaC & Configuration Drift (6 months of env vars, IAM policies, and security-group edits were never applied to the DR workspace).

Pre-Reserve DR Quotas & Capacity

Match vCPU/DB service quotas in the DR region and hold Capacity Reservations for critical core nodes.

Continuous Multi-Region IaC Sync

Deploy identical Terraform/Helm modules to both regions on every merge and run daily synthetic checks.

STAGE 03 · WHERE IT IS USEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

Where Multi-Region DR fits (and where Active-Active is required).

Choose DR when 1–60 minutes of failover downtime is acceptable and 2x active-active compute is unnecessary.

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
Good Candidate: Regulated SLAs with 5–60m RTOSurvives full regional outages at a fraction of active-active compute cost.Requires regular game-day failover drills and DNS/DB promotion automation.
Avoid: Zero-Second RTO (<30s) & RPO = 0Use Multi-Region Active-Active with synchronous consensus when zero downtime is mandatory.Active-active doubles baseline compute/DB spend and adds cross-region RTT.

STAGE 04 · HOW IT IS MANAGEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

Automated Failover Runbook & DNS Health Gate.

Automate DB replica promotion, ASG/Karpenter scale-up, and DNS cutover in a single idempotent runbook rather than manual console clicks during a P0.

# Terraform: Low-TTL Route53 Failover Record + Aurora Global Secondary
resource "aws_route53_record" "api_failover" {
  zone_id = aws_route53_zone.main.zone_id
  name    = "api.example.com"
  type    = "A"
  ttl     = 30  # Keep DNS TTL low so clients shift within 30-60s
  set_identifier = "secondary-dr"
  failover_routing_policy { type = "SECONDARY" }
  records = [aws_lb.dr_alb.dns_name]
}

STAGE 05 · ALTERNATIVESL6 · SCALE & TRADE-OFFS

Alternatives: Backup & Restore vs. Pilot Light vs. Warm Standby.

Every DR tier trades steady-state standby cost against RTO.

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
Backup & Restore (S3 Cross-Region)Costs ~5% overhead beyond storage.4–12 hour RTO rebuilding VPCs/clusters and restoring multi-TB snapshots.
Pilot Light (DB Replicating, Compute Off/Minimal)Core database stays warm; compute spins up on failover in 10–30 minutes.Vulnerable to cold-start capacity shortages if quotas aren't reserved.
Warm Standby (Scaled-Down Live Stack)1–5 minute RTO; continuous synthetic health checks prove config parity daily.Higher baseline compute cost (~20–30% of primary).
06
STAGE 06 · PRODUCTION ARCHITECTUREL7 · STAFF / PRINCIPAL

Warm Standby with Daily Synthetic Parity & Ransomware Vault.

Production DR architectures pair a Warm Standby secondary region (continuously receiving async DB/WAL replication and serving internal synthetic probes) with immutable S3 Object Lock backups in an isolated AWS/GCP account so even a compromised root credential or ransomware attack cannot wipe recovery snapshots.

Failover Execution

Automated DR Cutover & Old-Primary Fencing

Always fence the old primary before promoting the DR replica so a recovering region cannot create split-brain.
