STAGE 01 · WHAT IT ISL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

What are Bounded Contexts & Strategic Context Mapping.

Why this matters: The mechanism: understand how Bounded Contexts & Strategic Context Mapping works from first principles.

A Customer in Sales (lead score, pipeline stage) is fundamentally different from a Customer in Billing (tax ID, credit limit) or Shipping (geo-coordinates). Forcing all three into one class creates a brittle Big Ball of Mud.

A Bounded Context defines the explicit perimeter within which a specific Ubiquitous Language and domain model apply. Context Mapping documents how contexts integrate—technically (APIs, events, translators) and organizationally (Conway's Law)—using patterns like Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL), Open Host Service (OHS) + Published Language, Customer-Supplier, Conformist, and Shared Kernel.

Why Shared Schemas Break Teams (and How ACL + OHS Isolate Churn).

Why this matters: The problem: see what breaks without Why Shared Schemas Break Teams under real concurrency or scale.

Think of Why Shared Schemas Break Teams (and How ACL + OHS Isolate Churn) as the intuition layer for DDD Strategic Context Mapping: The problem: see what breaks without Why Shared Schemas Break Teams under real concurrency or scale.

Sharing one monolithic Order or Customer database table across 4+ teams creates semantic drift: Sales changes Account.status = 'ACTIVE' to include trial users, silently causing Billing to invoice free accounts. Explicit OHS/Published Language contracts upstream and ACL translators downstream isolate each context's internal model.

Published Language decouples Checkout from N consumers, while the ACL insulates Fulfillment from cryptic legacy ERP schemas.

Naive Solution
Use a single unpartitioned path with unbounded retries and synchronous global coordination.
Why It Fails
Global coordination serializes throughput under load, while unbounded retries amplify transient stalls into full outages.
Alternatives
Compare synchronous strict-consistency paths against bounded asynchronous replication, local caching, or partitioned ownership.

STAGE 04 · HOW IT IS MANAGEDL5 · APPLIED DESIGN

How to Enforce Boundaries in Modular Monoliths & Event Schemas.

Why this matters: Operational control: configure bounds, failure handling, and safe production defaults.

Bounded Contexts do not require separate microservices on day one.

Tools like Spring Modulith or ArchUnit enforce context boundaries inside a single deployable binary—blocking direct repository imports across packages while allowing in-process domain events.

How to Enforce Boundaries in Modular Monoliths & Event Schemas

// ArchUnit rule enforcing Bounded Context isolation inside a Modular Monolith (Java)
@ArchTest
static final ArchRule contexts_must_not_access_other_repositories =
    noClasses().that().resideInAPackage("..fulfillment..")
        .should().dependOnClassesThat().resideInAPackage("..checkout.infrastructure..");

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL)Low coupling; protects core domain from messy legacy/vendor DTOs.Extra translator boilerplate and mapping tests at the edge.
