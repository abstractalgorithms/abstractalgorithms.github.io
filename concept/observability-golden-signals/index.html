Signals.

The Four Golden Signals—Latency, Traffic, Errors, and Saturation—form the minimal, high-signal metric foundation for monitoring any user-facing distributed system. Measuring them with histograms (not averages) and separating successful vs. failed request latencies exposes tail regressions and capacity cliffs before outages hit.

STAGE 01 · WHAT IT ISL4 · FUNDAMENTALS

What are the Four Golden Signals.

Why this matters: The mechanism: understand how the Four Golden Signals works from first principles.

Coined in Google's Site Reliability Engineering book, the Four Golden Signals define the essential telemetry needed to understand user experience and system headroom: Latency (request duration, tracked via p50/p95/p99 histograms with 2xx split from fast-failing 5xx), Traffic (demand in RPS, messages/sec, or active sessions), Errors (explicit 5xx, implicit 200 OK fallback payloads, and policy violations like SLA timeouts), and Saturation (utilization of the most constrained resource—thread pools, DB connection queues, heap after GC, or CPU CFS throttling).

Two mnemonics operationalize these signals per layer: RED (Rate, Errors, Duration) for request-driven microservices, and USE (Utilization, Saturation, Errors) for hardware and infrastructure.

Engineering Decision Framework
Problem
How to implement Observability Golden Signals without hitting unbounded tail latency, state divergence, or operational fragility.
Naive Solution
Use a single unpartitioned path with unbounded retries and synchronous global coordination.
Why It Fails
Global coordination serializes throughput under load, while unbounded retries amplify transient stalls into full outages.
Alternatives
Compare synchronous strict-consistency paths against bounded asynchronous replication, local caching, or partitioned ownership.
Engineering Decision
If latency budgets leave no room for synchronous retries, instrument every service boundary with Prometheus/OTel histograms for Latency (p50/p95/p99, splitting 2xx from 5xx), Traffic (RPS), Errors (explicit + policy), and Saturation (the tightest resource bottleneck)—and alert on SLO error-budget burn rates rather than raw CPU spikes.
Trade-Offs
Gains predictable p99 latency and fault isolation in exchange for explicit bounding, observability, and slightly higher implementation complexity.

Histograms + Exemplars Over Averages

Compute histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket[5m]))) and attach OpenTelemetry trace_id exemplars so on-call jumps from a p99 spike straight to the slow trace.

Explicit + Implicit + Policy Errors

A degraded recommendation engine returning HTTP 200 with [] keeps 5xx at 0.0% while conversion plummets—emit explicit fallback counters (recommendation_fallback_total) into your Error SLI.

Where Golden Signals apply (and where they do not).

Why this matters: Scope boundary: identify where this pattern fits best and where simpler or async alternatives win.

Golden Signals belong at every synchronous and asynchronous service boundary—HTTP/gRPC gateways, message consumers, and database pools—while root-cause debugging and offline batch jobs rely on complementary telemetry.

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
Good Candidate: User-Facing Microservices & GatewaysProvides instant incident triage across Latency, Traffic, Errors, and Saturation to drive multi-window SLO burn alerts.Requires bounded metric labels (`service`, `route_template`, `status_class`) to prevent Prometheus cardinality explosions.
Good Candidate: Queue & Stream Consumers (Kafka/SQS)Maps Traffic to messages/sec and Saturation to consumer lag and partition rebalance frequency.Must exclude idle poll loops and healthchecks from latency histograms.
Avoid: Per-Request Root-Cause DebuggingUse distributed traces (with `trace_id` exemplars) and structured logs for line-level stack traces and `user_id` inspection.Adding `user_id` or unnormalized URLs (`/users/12345`) to Prometheus labels crashes TSDB memory.

Why this matters: Operational control: configure bounds, failure handling, and safe production defaults.

Instrument services with OpenTelemetry or Prometheus native/exponential histograms, exclude /healthz and /metrics from SLIs, bound label cardinality, and compute multi-window burn-rate rules in PromQL so pages fire only when monthly error budgets (e.g., 43.2 minutes at 99.9%) are genuinely threatened.

Design Trade-Off

How Golden Signals are instrumented and alerted

Before · Unbounded Coupling
  • No backpressure on spikes
  • Implicit shared state
  • Cascading tail latency
After · Bounded & Observable
  • Explicit capacity budgets
  • Isolated failure domains
  • Predictable p99 latency
# 1. Latency p99 for successful 2xx requests only (exclude fast-fail 5xx)
histogram_quantile(0.99,
  sum by (le, route_template) (
    rate(http_server_request_duration_seconds_bucket{service="payments",status_class="2xx"}[5m])
  )
)

# 2. Multi-window fast-burn SLO alert (14.4x burn consumes 2% of 30d budget in 1h)
(
  sum(rate(http_server_requests_total{service="payments",status_class="5xx"}[5m]))
  / sum(rate(http_server_requests_total{service="payments"}[5m])) > (14.4 * 0.001)
)
and
(
  sum(rate(http_server_requests_total{service="payments",status_class="5xx"}[1h]))
  / sum(rate(http_server_requests_total{service="payments"}[1h])) > (14.4 * 0.001)
)

Component Topology

Alternatives: Native Histograms vs. Fixed Buckets & SLO vs. Resource Alerts

ChoiceWhat it buysWhat it costs
OTel Exponential / Native HistogramsPreserves accurate p99 across shifting latency scales without manual bucket tuning or 20x label multiplication.Requires Prometheus 2.40+/Mimir native histogram support across the entire scrape pipeline.
Fixed-Bucket Prometheus HistogramsUniversally supported across legacy exporters and Grafana panels.Over-specified buckets (20+ `le` labels) multiply time-series cardinality by 20x; coarse buckets distort p99.
Symptom-Based SLO Alerts vs. Cause-Based Resource AlertsSLO burn alerts cut false-positive pages by 70%+ because high CPU without user impact does not wake engineers.Rapidly filling disks (92%+) still need proactive warning tickets before hitting an abrupt 100% error cliff.
