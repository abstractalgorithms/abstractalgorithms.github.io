HNSW Vector Indexing &
Approximate Nearest Neighbors.

HNSW organizes high-dimensional vectors into a hierarchy of proximity graphs—sparse long-range highways on top and a dense base graph on Layer 0—delivering O(log N) search at >98% recall.

The idea to keep with you

If model quality drifts from offline training baselines, tune M for graph memory/connectivity, efConstruction for build-time edge quality, and efSearch for query-time latency vs. recall.

TL;DR30-second simplified summary
▾

HNSW replaces O(N * D) flat scans with an O(log N * D) multi-layer proximity graph. Queries greedily descend from upper express layers to Layer 0, where a bounded efSearch beam finds top-K neighbors. Heuristic pruning maintains angular connectivity, while SQ8/PQ compression and periodic compaction keep RAM bounded and recall high.
