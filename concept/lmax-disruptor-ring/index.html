LMAX Disruptor &
Lock-Free Ring Buffers.

The LMAX Disruptor replaces lock-contended queues with a pre-allocated power-of-two ring buffer, single-writer/CAS sequence claiming, and 64-byte cache-line padding to eliminate GC pauses and false sharing.

The idea to keep with you

If lock contention or cache misses stall worker threads, pre-allocate a 2^N ring buffer, claim slots with seq & (N - 1), pad sequence cursors to 64 bytes, and gate producers on the slowest consumer sequence.

TL;DR30-second simplified summary
▾

The LMAX Disruptor eliminates lock contention, false sharing, and GC churn via a pre-allocated 2^N ring buffer, two-phase CAS slot claiming, and 64-byte padded Sequence cursors. SequenceBarriers coordinate parallel and DAG consumer stages while WaitStrategies trade CPU for tail latency.
